While walking back from class, two friends found a small silver key
beside a locked maintenance door. A note attached to it read,
“Only open this after sunset.” They thought it was a prank until they heard three
knocks coming from the other side.

When the sun finally set,
they slid the key into the lock and slowly opened the door.
Inside, they found an empty room with a ringing phone and a photograph of themselves standing outside the door just minutes earlier.

One of them answered the phone and heard their roommate laughing on the other end.
He stepped out from behind the door with a camera, and they realized the mysterious key and photograph
had been part of an elaborate prank.
